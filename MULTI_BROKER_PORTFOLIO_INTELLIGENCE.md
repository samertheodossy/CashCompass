# Multi-Broker Portfolio Intelligence — Architecture Direction

**Status:** ROADMAP ONLY — **do not implement** until the current Connected
Accounts / Plaid / Apply milestone cluster is complete and committed.

**Purpose:** Preserve product direction across chat and tool transitions. This
document is architecture and sequencing guidance, not a schema specification.
Inspect live workbook schemas (`SYS - Investment Activity`, `SYS - Investment
Holdings`, Robinhood adapter code) before any future implementation.

---

## Product direction

CashCompass must evolve from **investment-account balance tracking** into a
**normalized multi-broker portfolio intelligence system**.

Primary brokerage sources expected:

- Robinhood (existing foundation)
- E*TRADE
- M1
- Schwab
- future brokerages as approved

**One canonical model.** Do not build separate brokerage-specific portfolio
models. Every supported source normalizes into the same CashCompass investment
contracts regardless of ingestion path.

---

## Robinhood protection (non-negotiable default policy)

Robinhood represents the owner's intentional **passive-income / long-term**
strategy.

**Default account policy:** `PROTECTED` / `DO_NOT_SELL_FOR_CASH_FUNDING`

Robinhood holdings must **not** be proposed for liquidation by the cash-funding
optimizer unless the owner explicitly changes policy later.

Robinhood data still contributes to:

- total portfolio visibility
- passive-income calculations
- allocation and concentration analysis
- dividend/distribution projections
- long-term investment planning

---

## Primary cash-funding / portfolio-optimization accounts

Initial optimization **candidates** (subject to future owner policy):

- E*TRADE
- M1
- Schwab

These portfolios may eventually support analysis of:

- what to hold / sell and which tax lots to sell
- cash generated by a sale and estimated realized gain/loss
- short-term vs long-term tax exposure
- income lost by selling and income gained by reinvesting
- concentration and allocation impact
- funding upcoming CashCompass payments and new investments
- improving sustainable passive income

**Recommendation-only.** No automatic trade execution in any phase listed here.
Execution requires a separately reviewed and approved future milestone.

---

## Ingestion must be source-agnostic

Use the **best reliable source per brokerage**. Plaid is optional, not required.

| Source type | Notes |
|---|---|
| Plaid Investments | When accurate and permitted |
| Brokerage CSV exports | Expected and acceptable when superior |
| Transaction/history exports | Preferred when CSV gives richer activity |
| Manually reviewed structured imports | Fail-closed, provenance-backed |
| Other approved structured integrations | Separate security/consent review |

**Architecture seam (mandatory):**

```
SOURCE ADAPTER → NORMALIZATION → CANONICAL CASHCOMPASS INVESTMENT DATA
```

Provider-specific schemas must never become the canonical model.

This extends — does not replace — the reusable import direction in `RFP-6c`
and the Robinhood-first adapter proven under `RFP-6a`.

---

## Existing CashCompass foundation (extend, do not replace)

Preserve and extend the **Robinhood model** and related SYS sheets:

- **SYS - Investment Activity** — normalized transaction/activity ledger
- **SYS - Investment Holdings** — derived or imported holdings view

Holdings-style concepts already in use include (non-exhaustive):

- Investment Id, Account Name, As Of Date, Ticker, Quantity
- Total Buy Cost, Sale Proceeds, Dividends Received
- Weekly Recurring Buy, Last Activity Price, Activity Count, Updated At

Investment Activity is the underlying history ledger. **Review actual column
contracts in the workbook before implementation.**

`INPUT - Investments` remains account-total authority until a separately
approved authority migration.

---

## Canonical activity requirement

For every supported brokerage, normalize transaction history sufficient to
reconstruct positions **where source data permits**.

Evaluate support for at minimum:

- BUY, SELL, DIVIDEND, DISTRIBUTION, REINVESTMENT
- TRANSFER_IN, TRANSFER_OUT, SPLIT, FEE, INTEREST
- recurring investment activity
- brokerage-specific rows requiring explicit canonical classification

Each normalized activity retains provenance for:

- replay protection and deduplication
- account identity and security identity
- activity date, quantity, price, gross amount, fees (when available)
- source, source transaction key, import timestamp

**Do not invent missing values.**

---

## Holdings requirement

Derive or import a **current holdings view** per account/security. Evaluate at
minimum:

- ticker/security identifier, quantity, current/reference price, market value
- aggregate purchase cost / cost basis, unrealized gain/loss
- realized sale proceeds, dividends/distributions received
- recurring investment amount where applicable
- activity count, last activity price/date, as-of timestamp, source freshness

The canonical holdings model must behave identically whether data came from
Robinhood, E*TRADE, M1, Schwab, Plaid, or CSV.

---

## Purchase / tax-lot requirement (critical)

**Aggregate holdings alone are insufficient** for the future optimizer.

Where source data permits, preserve **purchase/tax-lot** detail:

- brokerage account, security/ticker, acquisition date
- quantity acquired, remaining quantity, purchase price
- original and adjusted cost basis (when provided)
- current value, unrealized gain/loss, holding period
- short-term vs long-term status, source/provenance

Do **not** synthesize tax lots when the brokerage does not provide sufficient
information. Mark optimization confidence/limitations explicitly when lot data
is missing.

Aligns with future **RFP-7** tax-lot-aware sale planning; this milestone
defines the data foundation RFP-7 requires.

---

## Portfolio intelligence (recommendation engine)

After normalized data is accurate, add a **recommendation engine** that answers
questions such as:

- "What should I sell to generate $25,000 for upcoming CashCompass payments?"
- "What should I sell to fund another investment?"
- "Which lots minimize unnecessary tax cost?"
- "How does this sale affect passive income and concentration?"

**Do not optimize solely for lowest taxes.** Objectives include:

- required cash, estimated tax impact, ST vs LT gains, harvested losses
- portfolio allocation, concentration, liquidity
- passive income lost vs future passive income gained
- recurring investment strategy and owner account protection rules

CashCompass **recommends**; it does **not** place trades in these phases.

---

## Passive-income model

Calculate portfolio income across supported investment accounts:

- dividends/distributions received, recurring and trailing income
- projected annual/monthly income, yield on value and on cost where useful
- income lost by proposed sale, income gained by proposed reinvestment

Goal: **current portfolio income → proposed action → projected income after action.**

Do not equate headline yield with sustainable after-tax income.

---

## Account policy / protection model

Design for account-level policy labels such as:

- `PROTECTED`, `NORMAL`, `PREFERRED_SOURCE`, `DO_NOT_SELL`
- `INCOME_STRATEGY`, `GROWTH_STRATEGY`

Exact enum and UI are future design work.

| Account | Initial known policy |
|---|---|
| Robinhood | Protected long-term / passive-income; **exclude from normal cash-funding sell recommendations** |
| E*TRADE, M1, Schwab | Candidate optimization accounts; owner policy TBD |

---

## Data quality (mandatory)

Every imported brokerage dataset must record:

- source, account identity, import timestamp, source as-of date when available
- dedupe/replay strategy, missing-data handling, confidence/completeness indicators

Never silently manufacture: quantity, cost basis, acquisition date, tax lot,
dividend, price, or market value.

## Phased roadmap

| Phase | Scope | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Multi-Broker Data Foundation** | Inspect Robinhood canonical model; define Activity / Holdings / Tax Lot contracts; identify gaps without breaking Robinhood; define adapter contract | **Next Investment milestone after Connected/Apply** |
| **2 — E*TRADE** | Best export/provider data (CSV if superior to Plaid); normalize activity, holdings, tax lots; reconcile totals; owner runtime validation | After Phase 1 |
| **3 — M1** | Same normalized model and reconciliation discipline | After Phase 2 |
| **4 — Schwab** | Same normalized model and reconciliation discipline | After Phase 3 |
| **5 — Portfolio Intelligence** | Allocation, concentration, gains/losses, holding periods, tax-lot analysis, passive-income and liquidity analysis | After broker data proven |
| **6 — Cash-Funding Optimizer** | Cash requirement → eligible accounts → candidate lots → tax/allocation/income ranking → sell recommendations; **Robinhood excluded by default** | Recommendation-only |
| **7 — Reinvestment Optimizer** | Excess cash / proceeds → target allocation, passive-income objectives, recurring-buy strategy → ranked buy recommendations | Recommendation-only |
| **8 — Execution** | Trade execution | **Explicitly out of scope** until separately approved |

## Safety boundaries (all phases)

- No automatic trading
- No silent authority switch for investment totals
- No raw provider credential or token retention in workbook
- No provider-specific canonical fields
- Robinhood protected from default sell-for-cash recommendations
- Fail closed on missing tax-lot or basis data
